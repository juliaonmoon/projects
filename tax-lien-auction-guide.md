# U.S. Tax Lien Auction Guide

_Last updated: August 11, 2026_

This is a practical reference for buying **property-tax lien certificates** (not ordinary mortgage foreclosures). Rules and schedules are county-specific and can change, so verify the county page before funding an account or bidding.

## 1. Florida

### Best starting sites

- Orange County Tax Collector — Tax Certificate & Deed Sales: https://octaxcol.com/taxes/about-property-tax/tax-certificate-deed-sales/
- Leon County Tax Collector — Annual Tax Sale / investor information: https://www.leontaxcollector.net/Services/Property-Taxes/Annual-Tax-Sale
- Florida statutes, Chapter 197: https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/

### Schedule

Florida tax collectors conduct the annual tax-certificate sale **on or before June 1** (subject to statutory timing rules). Orange County's 2026 online sale closed June 1.

After the annual auction, some counties offer **county-held certificates** for direct purchase. Check the county tax collector's investor page.

### Interest

- Maximum certificate rate: **18% per year**.
- Annual auction is a **reverse auction**: bidders compete by accepting a lower interest rate.
- A zero-rate bid can earn zero interest.

### Florida rule to remember: HOMESTEAD vs NON-HOMESTEAD

This is the most important Florida screening field for a lien investor who might eventually pursue a deed.

If a certificate later goes to a tax-deed sale:

- **Non-homestead:** opening bid generally consists of the certificate/tax amounts, other required taxes/certificates, interest, fees and costs.
- **Homestead:** Florida adds **50% of the property's latest assessed value** to the tax-deed opening bid.

Example:

- Assessed value: $400,000
- Your lien: $8,000
- Other taxes/costs: $3,000
- Homestead addition: $200,000
- Approximate opening bid: **$211,000**

If no one bids and you are unwilling to fund the homestead addition, you do not simply receive your original certificate money back. The property can move to the **lands available for taxes** process. Leon County explicitly warns that if such property remains unpurchased long enough to escheat to the county, the applicant's investment can be lost.

**Screening preference:** If the goal is primarily interest income with a tolerable deed fallback, prioritize **non-homestead + usable property + property value comfortably above lien exposure**.

### Canadian / foreign bidder issue

Florida can be workable for Canadians, but county requirements matter. Leon and Martin County state that foreign bidders may participate with an **ITIN** and **W-8** documentation. Some counties require payment through ACH from a **U.S. bank account**.

Before registering, confirm the exact foreign-bidder rules of the county you plan to use.

## 2. Arizona — Pima County

### Sites

- Pima County Treasurer tax-lien page: https://www.to.pima.gov/taxLienSale/
- Pima County Treasurer home/current notices: https://www.to.pima.gov/
- Auction platform: https://www.realauction.com/

### 2026 schedule

- Registration: **Feb. 2–Feb. 19, 2026**
- Annual auction: **Feb. 26, 2026**
- Over-the-counter / **New Assignment** lien purchases: **Apr. 1–Dec. 15, 2026**

This means **Pima County still has a 2026 route to buy liens after the annual auction** through its New Assignment inventory.

### Interest / bidding

- Auction begins at **16%**.
- Investors bid the interest rate downward in **1% increments**.
- **0% is allowed**.
- Interest on a redeemed auction certificate is based on the winning bid rate under Arizona rules.

### Important rules

- You are buying a lien, not the real estate.
- Pima says the lien generally must have been outstanding for **3 years** before a judicial foreclosure action can be initiated.
- Pima has a **Single Simultaneous Bidding Entity Rule** at competitive auctions.
- Research over-the-counter liens especially carefully: inventory left after the main auction may include less desirable parcels.

## 3. Colorado — Jefferson County

### Sites

- Jefferson County Internet Tax Lien Sale: https://www.jeffco.us/2438/Internet-Sale-Information
- Registration: https://www.jeffco.us/2434/Registration-for-the-Tax-Lien-Sale
- Pre-sale / return information: https://www.jeffco.us/2432/Pre-Sale-Procedures
- Auction provider: SRI / Zeus Auction

### 2026 schedule

- Registration begins **October 2026**
- Auction: **November 2026** (exact date to be posted by the county)

### Interest / bidding

Colorado differs from Florida. The statutory certificate interest rate is set by formula; Jefferson County states the 2025 sale rate was **14%**.

The major trap is the **premium**:

- Investors may bid **above** the delinquent-tax amount.
- Interest is earned on the **certificate face amount**, not the premium.
- **Premium is not refunded.**

Therefore calculate effective yield using total cash invested, not merely the statutory rate.

### Canadian issue

Jefferson County currently says **only bidders with a U.S. taxpayer ID number may participate**. A Canadian investor should confirm whether an ITIN satisfies the county's current registration requirements before assuming eligibility.

## 4. Indiana

### Sites

- SRI Tax Sale Services: https://www.sriservices.com/
- Zeus Auction: https://www.zeusauction.com/
- Example official county page — Scott County: https://www.scottcounty.in.gov/291/Tax-Sales
- Example official county page — Noble County: https://www.in.gov/counties/noble/offices/treasurer/

### 2026 examples

- Scott County: **Sept. 9, 2026**
- Noble County: **Oct. 5, 2026**

Indiana schedules vary by county, so use SRI/Zeus to identify upcoming auctions and then verify the county Treasurer/Auditor page.

### Important rule

A regular Indiana tax sale gives the successful bidder a **tax sale certificate / lien**, not immediate ownership. The owner generally has about **one year to redeem**. If not redeemed, the certificate holder must follow statutory notice and court procedures to obtain a tax deed.

Commissioners' certificate sales can have different terms; some counties state a **90-day redemption period** for those sales.

## 5. Iowa

### Sites

- Example official county — Johnson County: https://johnsoncountyiowa.gov/treasurer/tax-sale-information
- Example official county — Linn County: https://www.linncountyiowa.gov/208/Property-Tax-Forms
- Example official county — Iowa County: https://iowacounty.iowa.gov/treasurer/tax_sale/

### Schedule

Iowa tax sales are generally held annually on the **third Monday in June**.

2026 example:

- Johnson County: **June 15, 2026**

### Important rules

- Many Iowa sales are still **county/in-person**, not one statewide online portal.
- Pre-registration and bidder tax ID are required.
- Iowa has its own detailed certificate/redemption and deed-notice process; missed deadlines can destroy the value of a certificate.

For a Canadian investor seeking easy online access, Iowa is **less convenient** than Florida or Pima County, Arizona.

## 6. Illinois

### Starting site

- Illinois County Treasurers' Association: https://www.illinoistreasurers.org/

Use the association/county directory to find each county's Treasurer and tax-sale calendar.

### 2026 examples

- Coles County: **Oct. 23, 2026**
- Stark County: **Oct. 28, 2026**
- Rock Island County: **Dec. 29, 2026**
- Monroe County: **Jan. 14, 2027** for 2025 taxes payable in 2026

### Important rules

Illinois is a tax-lien/certificate market but is procedurally more complex than Florida or Arizona. County registration requirements may include:

- bidder registration deadlines,
- fees/deposits,
- letter of intent,
- proof/letter of credit,
- strict redemption and deed-notice procedure.

Treat Illinois as an **advanced** market until its penalty-bid and redemption mechanics are fully understood.

## 7. Maryland

### Sites

- Howard County Tax Sale portal: https://taxsale.howardcountymd.gov/Public/Home.aspx
- Montgomery County tax-lien purchaser information: https://www3.montgomerycountymd.gov/311/

### 2026 schedules

Howard County:

- Registration opened May 21, 2026
- Registration closed June 3
- Tax sale: **June 10, 2026**

Montgomery County:

- Tax sale is held annually on the **second Monday in June**.

### Important rule: occupied status matters

Maryland is another place where owner occupancy can matter. Montgomery County states that lien-release / foreclosure timing differs between **non-owner-occupied** and **owner-occupied** parcels.

That makes occupancy an important screening field, similar in spirit (though legally different) from Florida's homestead screen.

## 8. New Jersey

### Starting source

- NJ Department of Community Affairs — Tax Collection Resources / New Jersey Tax Lien and Sale Information: https://nj.gov/dca/dlgs/programs/tax_collector.shtml

New Jersey tax sales are primarily **municipal**, so there is no single statewide auction date or one universal portal. Use the DCA resource page, identify municipalities of interest, then check that municipality's tax collector for its sale notice and auction method.

### Important rule

Do not assume an advertised municipal tax certificate gives quick access to the property. Redemption and foreclosure of the right to redeem are separate legal stages.

# Screening checklist before bidding

For every lien, record these fields before committing money:

1. **State + county + parcel ID**
2. **Lien amount / total cash required**
3. **Winning interest rate or statutory return**
4. **Expected effective yield** after premium and fees
5. **Property assessed value and estimated market value**
6. **Property type** — normal house/condo/commercial/usable land vs tiny strip/common area/problem parcel
7. **Homestead / owner-occupied status**
8. **Other outstanding tax certificates / subsequent taxes**
9. **Mortgage and other title interests** — do not assume every interest disappears
10. **Environmental / code / HOA / municipal issues**
11. **Redemption period**
12. **Earliest date you can begin deed/foreclosure proceedings**
13. **Exact notices/legal steps required from certificate holder**
14. **Certificate expiration date**
15. **Worst-case question:** If the owner never redeems and nobody else wants this parcel, would I still want to own it at the total amount required?
